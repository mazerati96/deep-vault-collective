NOVEL — BOOK ONE

Project Star Spear

Outerversal Stories — The Star Spear Series
SYNOPSIS

The world had run out of power and one of the most advanced societies in the system is on the brink of collapse. Akareo rose to the ranks of a marshal in a short time for his species. Not only were they well connected within the waywalkers path that granted them a superior intellect, they had also become the councils right hand man when discussing the secrets of the world. That only bolstered when they told him they were almost out of power. The planets core had run nearly dry from their constant use of the material over time but they had found another source. Another planet close to them. They had sent a colony but they’d since claimed independence from their system. With a heavy heart they tell Akareo he must quash this rebellion and siphon the planets new fuel for their own. Will Akareo crush the rebellion to save his people? Or join the sympathisers in their ideals?

NOVEL — BOOK TWO

Project Fallen Nebulae

Outerversal Stories — The Star Spear Series
SYNOPSIS

The world had run out of power and one of the most advanced societies in the system was on the brink of collapse. Tai Djex was a grunt in the star fleet of his homeworld Uberix. Tai had no love for his government or his people so when the high council petitioned for volunteers to leave to another world for colonization, he had no qualms whatsoever. The journey was long but eventually they landed on Harik-2 with a disturbing message. To mine the specific element there and send it back to the homeworld for ‘study’. After some inquiries Tai had learned that his homeworld was running out of natural power. Swiftly he organized a rebellion and took the planets home for his own. Effectively starting one of the biggest galactic wars in his time, will Tai succeed? Or crumble under the weight of star steel?

NOVEL — BOOK THREE

Project Clash of Wills

Outerversal Stories — The Star Spear Series
SYNOPSIS

The world had run out of power and one of the most advanced societies in the system was on the brink of collapse, and the stars were now at war. Two powers, one as old as time and one newly sprung into the world clash over their inability to coexist. Akareo attempts to make peace with the rebel factions but Tai is having none of it. The rebellion is free of the coalitions oppression and harsh treatment and wants the open air of a new start for their families. As worlds collide will the iron fisted Akareo win out over the green Tai Djex? Or will the rebel leader strike a decisive enough blow to force a retreat?
