Neon Ghosts

SYNOPSIS

Quin Lorik hadn’t ever failed a job until his assassination of the president of the state of Hanzuk went sideways.

Quin had started his work as a hired gun at an early age. With his growth came experience and seeing the world of cybernetics for what it really was. Dark. Unforgiving. And ruthless. He forewent most social attachments for the pursuit of money, and it wasn’t until the botched assassination that his life began to change.

With a crew behind his back finally breaking down his social walls, will Quin change to go against the flows of Sey City? Or drown in the depths of the loss of humanity.
