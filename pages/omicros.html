SYNOPSIS

The world went to shit about a hundred years ago. Ain’t been the same since I’m told. I’m Ros. A lone pilgrim that’s been making my way across the land of Tenshin for about twenty years. Left my ma and pa behind because they weren’t nothin to me.

This land’s been harsh to me. Cannibals eatin people. Bandits ridin dragons. Government hold up with their swords and bows in one spot hopin to preserve what was. Whole place is fucked.

But… I gotta keep treckin on. Gotta keep movin. If I get to where I wanna go, I might be able to fix what happened. Fix the calamity that broke the damn sphere.
